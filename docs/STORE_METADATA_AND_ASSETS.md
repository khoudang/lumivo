## 3. Privacy Highlights (Bullet Points for Store Copy)

- **Zero Data Collection:** Lumivo does not collect, log, track, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or usage statistics.
- **100% Offline-First Architecture:** Operates entirely locally on your device without communicating with external cloud servers or remote APIs.
- **No Account Required:** Enjoy immediate full functionality without registration, email verification, or sign-in walls.
- **No Hardware Dependencies:** Explore an authentic smart home experience without purchasing physical Zigbee, Matter, BLE, or Wi-Fi bridges.
- **Sanitized Backup & Recovery:** Export and import local JSON backups with automated credential and secret redaction.
- **Complete User Data Autonomy:** Perform full factory resets or clear activity logs with a single tap.
